How it works

09:30

Everyone gets a card

One DM, one form: yesterday, today, blockers, mood. Edit until the deadline; the posted card updates in place.

As they answer

Answers land in one thread

Each answer is a card under today’s thread in your space. Away people are never nagged or counted as missing.

11:30

The wrap-up tells the truth

How many submitted and exactly who didn’t. Blockers open as items you can tag, acknowledge and resolve.

Everything a standup bot should do. Nothing it shouldn’t.

Away-aware

Skip today, vacation mode and Google Calendar OOO sync. Away people are neither nagged nor counted.

Blockers that follow up

Tag teammates, acknowledge, post updates, resolve. Daily nudges until acknowledged; escalation when stale.

Per-person timezones

Prompts go out at 09:30 in each person’s own zone. Late submissions are flagged, not lost.

Insights built in

Participation, mood and blocker trends. Weekly digest, CSV export, signed webhooks.

Enterprise SSO, no tax

Google sign-in, SAML 2.0 and SCIM provisioning — all in the MIT core.

MCP server

Your team’s AI assistants can read standups and work blockers. No model runs inside AsyncUp.

Yours to run. Yours to keep.

One ~300 MB container. Embedded SQLite by default, or bring your own PostgreSQL with one variable. No telemetry, no account with us, no plan to upgrade to.
